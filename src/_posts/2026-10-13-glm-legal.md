  GLM-5.3-Flash foundation with mid-training on expanded legal corpora and
  reinforcement learning across tens of thousands of realistic legal

  all-pass rate on Harvey LAB across 24 practice areas, 12.5 points above the
  base model, plus parity with frontier closed models on LegalBench and
  PLawBench, evaluations Vals says it ran independently. The post shows

  firms to apply for beta API access and try it in the AutoClaw desktop agent.

Most commenters land on mandatory human accountability, because a lawyer can be
sanctioned or disbarred while a model cannot. They argue disclosure of AI use
matters, pointing to a 500 dollar AI-generated client email that conflated key
details as proof firms already bill full rates for unchecked output. The
sharpest objection is that disclosure misses the point, because courts should
vet citations and reasoning whether the error came from a model, a paralegal, or
Westlaw. A second fault line is openness: several argue GLM-Legal is dead on
arrival for legal work without open weights around 14 thousand dollars in
self-host hardware, since firms will not send client confidences to z.ai
servers.
